# ParaCFD

ParaCFD is a C++20/CUDA/Qt6 aerodynamic CFD application for stabilized paraglider geometry imported from STEP. It treats canopy skins, ribs, diagonals, and panels as zero-thickness, two-sided impermeable fabric; fluid exists on both sides, intentional CAD openings remain open, and no solid-volume filling or artificial extrusion is used.

## Physical model

The intended workflow is one-way coupling:

```
XPBD equilibrium -> STEP export -> ParaCFD preprocessing -> static CFD run
```

OpenCascade remains isolated in the geometry layer. STEP faces are tessellated in SI metres, with source-face provenance retained on every triangle. Triangle winding defines that triangle's local minus-to-plus normal only. Reversing winding swaps the pressure sides and normal together, leaving the physical pressure force invariant:

```
F = (p_minus - p_plus) A n
```

Missing triangles are openings. There is no separate interior-air material: ram-air cells, crossports, and the external flow contain the same fluid.

## Geometry preprocessing

- `TriMesh` stores positions, indices, source STEP face IDs, and optional per-corner UV coordinates.
- `TriangleBvh` is a static double-precision CPU BVH supporting cell AABB queries, segment intersections, nearest-surface queries, and distance queries.
- Runtime geometry is placed before BVH construction. Normals use the affine inverse transpose.
- CAD preprocessing is static. CUDA timesteps do not traverse CAD, rebuild topology, or intersect triangles.

The OpenCascade linear deflection is exposed in the paraglider configuration. The current default is 2 mm, which is still substantially finer than the initial 62.5 mm CFD surface cells and avoids unnecessary million-triangle previews. It should eventually be derived from finest CFD spacing.

## Grid and fields

The new grid path is static block-structured Cartesian AMR with refinement ratio 2:1. A brick has uniform cells and compact metadata: level, integer coordinate, origin, spacing, six same-level neighbours, parent, children, and boundary/embedded-boundary flags. Per-level integer-coordinate hash tables locate bricks without tree traversal or binary searches.

Fields use pooled, contiguous Structure-of-Arrays storage per AMR level. Production `Real` is `float`; `PARACFD_VALIDATION_FP64=ON` changes the new numerical path to `double` for validation. Geometry preprocessing and global validation calculations may still use double precision. A GPU-resident per-level coordinate hash locates the finest brick and local cell directly.

The implemented AMR path includes same-level halo exchange, ratio-two restriction/prolongation, aperture-aware flux matching, hierarchy balancing, and one matrix-free composite pressure operator spanning all active levels. Coarse/fine faces are compact four-tile connections; ordinary same-level faces remain implicit structured stencils. After transport, every compact tile gathers its collocated fine MAC velocity; after projection, corrected tiles scatter to the fine faces and their aperture-weighted mean is written to the coarse face.

`ExternalAeroCore` owns the static hierarchy/topology and persistent device fields. Its current timestep is bounded MacCormack AMR advection with RK2 characteristics, explicit molecular/Smagorinsky diffusion, external boundary conditions, compact EB-aperture transport, conservative coarse/fine synchronization, then the composite projection. A GPU max reduction over physical faces of active bricks chooses one global timestep from the finest spacing and current regular-flow velocity; ghost storage and covered coarse bricks are excluded, and only the scalar result returns to the host. Compact EB transport bounds its own local Courant/diffusion update, so a negligible geometric sliver cannot throttle the whole domain. Backtraces locate the finest active brick through the GPU hash. Trilinear and diffusion stencils cross same-level brick boundaries without clamping. At 2:1 transitions, staggered face values and cell-centred eddy viscosity use one-sided linear prolongation; the reverse MacCormack correction retains the bounded forward RK2 value if its trace changes lattice. Every level reads common old-time and forward states before correction/commit. Static preprocessing marks face centres within 2.5 local cells of fabric and stores six one-byte same-side link bits. Marked faces use bounded minmod-limited linear transport only when the complete upwind/downwind stencil is connected on the same fluid side, with a first-order donor fallback at sheet edges and unavailable level/boundary links; diffusion is likewise restricted to open links. Far faces retain MacCormack/LES. This prevents opposite-side stencil data without per-step triangle traversal while reducing fabric-band diffusion in smooth tangential flow.

## Zero-thickness embedded boundaries

Regular cells retain implicit Cartesian topology. Only cells intersected by fabric receive compact irregular records:

- fluid fragments with independent pressure state, volume, centroid, and parent cell;
- fragment connections through open fluid;
- Cartesian-face apertures with area, centroid, and the fluid fragments they join;
- fabric surface patches with source triangle/face IDs, area, centroid, normal, and plus/minus fragments.

A smooth membrane cut produces two fluid fragments and no connection through fabric. Fabric exactly coincident with a Cartesian face becomes a blocked cut face between two full-volume regular cells; partial coverage is reconstructed into separate connected apertures. Complex finest-level cells use a configurable deterministic local fluid-connectivity graph whose edges are blocked by exact BVH intersections. This supports more than two fragments at ribs, sheet endings, and junctions without inside/outside filling or cross-fabric merging; any topology still unresolved is a hard preprocessing failure.

Independent EB aperture-normal velocities are advanced on a compact same-side graph. Structured MAC faces adjacent to each fragment supply the carrier state; their staggered momentum volume is the sum of the two adjacent fluid half-volumes, including same-side fragment merges, rather than an assumed `h^3`. Static preprocessing links nearest same-axis apertures into directed chains; complete upstream/upstream2/downstream chains use a minmod-limited MUSCL update, while sheet endings, junctions, and incomplete chains retain the bounded first-order donor fallback. Each velocity component is collocated at its area-weighted aperture/carrier sample position. Static weighted least-squares matrices then reconstruct the row-major `du_i/dx_j` tensor from the physical displacement between connected same-side samples. CPU preprocessing stores a symmetric pseudoinverse per fragment/component; production FP32 rejects eigenmodes with condition number above `1e4` instead of amplifying an unsupported seam direction. The symmetric tensor supplies `|S| = sqrt(2 Sij Sij)` to compact-region Smagorinsky viscosity. Molecular diffusion and that eddy viscosity are applied before projection. The combined explicit update is limited to the extrema of its actual same-side stencil, so transport cannot invent a new local maximum or minimum; there is no arbitrary absolute velocity cap, and the subsequent pressure projection remains free to create physically required extrema. The graph contains only actual fluid connections, so it cannot transport through fabric and it does not turn the regular domain into CSR.

Every surface patch also contributes to a separate compact two-sided fabric-wall work list, including a sheet exactly coincident with a Cartesian face and therefore having no compact aperture edge. Each side uses its own fluid-control-volume centroid and pressure DOF. The production wall path evaluates the arbitrary-orientation tangential velocity at each patch side and solves Spalding's smooth-wall law continuously through the viscous and log layers. The requested `rho A u_tau^2` impulse is divided by each component's exact staggered dual-volume weight, then distributed to its incident regular carriers and compact apertures; the two fabric sides never share a state. The equal-and-opposite reaction is retained per surface patch with CAD triangle/face provenance. Manufactured low/high-Re residual, dissipation, reversed-winding, and fluid-impulse/fabric-reaction gates pass in FP32 and FP64; the impulse mismatch is about `9.7e-7` in the FP32 fixture and `1.2e-13` in FP64. The older implicit molecular no-slip sink remains as a separately tested validation path but is no longer applied on top of the production wall model.

Small fragments below `min_volume_fraction` (default 0.25 of a Cartesian cell) are conservatively merged through suitable open apertures to a larger same-side control volume. Fabric is never a legal merge connection. A 0.05 experiment retained substantially stiffer PlanB compact states, so 0.25 remains the stability-oriented default while resolution studies quantify its geometric influence. Apertures below `min_aperture_area_fraction * h^2` (default `1e-4`) are finite-resolution clipping slivers: preprocessing removes the matching velocity and pressure connection together and reports their count and cumulative atlas area. This threshold does not close a resolved CAD opening, and can be set to zero for geometry studies. Isolated sealed fluid components retain their pressure states and volumes; the composite solve assigns one explicit pressure gauge to every component not connected to the pressure-reference outlet.

## Pressure projection and aerodynamic loads

The matrix-free finite-volume pressure operator uses actual control-volume volumes and coefficients proportional to open area divided by centre distance. Regular/regular faces retain a structured fast path; EB apertures, blocked coincident faces, and 2:1 coarse/fine tiles are compact special cases. A sparse cross-brick atlas prevents brick boundaries from becoming physical walls and augments one composite pressure graph spanning every level. CPU reference and FP32/FP64 CUDA operator implementations share the topology. A persistent GPU projection performs divergence assembly, RHS formation, one composite PCG solve, pressure scatter, and conservative regular/special-flux correction without bulk field transfers. The PCG preconditioner combines the fine diagonal with an additive level-zero Galerkin aggregate solve; compact nonlocal aggregate edges retain conservative fragment-merge topology. Each active fluid component that cannot reach the pressure outlet receives a deterministic gauge. GPU boundary kernels prescribe +X freestream, use an X-max pressure reference/zero-gradient velocity, and apply the same free-slip far-field condition at both Y and both Z boundaries; there is no ground branch.

Every owned composite surface patch retains its global plus/minus pressure DOFs and CAD triangle/face provenance. Surface pressure results retain `p_plus`, `p_minus`, `delta_p`, the corresponding Cp values, and pressure force per source triangle. Smooth-wall shear force is accumulated independently per triangle and whole-wing force/moment; pressure, viscous, and total D/S/L components remain separately available. Whole-wing accumulation uses double precision. With freestream along +X, drag, side, and lift axes are +X, +Y, and +Z. Coefficients are reported only when the user supplies a positive reference area.

## Visualization collision

The Qt/OpenGL viewer still displays the STEP surface. Flow particles and tracers can use the placed triangle BVH for segment-crossing tests and cannot pass through fabric. This does not use an inside-solid or parity classification. The mesh renderer accepts per-source-triangle delta-Cp through an OpenGL shader-storage buffer indexed by primitive ID, so shared CAD vertices do not smear results across fabric panels. A dedicated worker now advances `ExternalAeroCore`, throttles surface/conservation downloads, publishes delta-Cp automatically, and reports separate pressure, smooth-wall skin-friction, and total loads plus CFL, regular/compact-EB velocity maxima, projection convergence, divergence/flux diagnostics, and persistent GPU memory. The scalar plane samples the finest active AMR brick at every display vertex from the throttled per-level host snapshot; its two axis-dependent dimensions are derived from the domain extent and finest active cell spacing rather than a fixed square resolution, with a bounded visualization-only allocation cap. Pressure samples inside cut cells select the actual local fluid-fragment DOF rather than the inactive parent Cartesian slot, so the two sides of a zero-thickness sheet remain independent in the slice. The overlay states the current sample dimensions and finest `h`. Arrows and tracers intentionally retain a cheaper coarse 3-D display field. The default camera fits the placed wing; `Fit Wing` and `Fit Domain` explicitly separate close aerodynamic inspection from inspection of the longer downstream wake volume. Live controls expose animated 3-D/2-D arrows, a stationary regular vector lattice, and tracer mode/density/length/width/boring-filter settings. `2D Section` looks normal to the active slice, clips the camera-side half of the STEP surface, disables tracers, and selects the black vector lattice over the scalar plane. The scalar plane never writes depth, so 2-D arrows and tracers remain fully visible over it while the opaque STEP canopy still occludes flow glyphs on its far side. Editors ignore ordinary wheel input so scrolling the compact settings dock cannot silently change simulation or visualization values.

The purpose-built paraglider window controls density/viscosity, face-bbox domain margins, base spacing/level count, surface/wing/wake refinement, CFL, Smagorinsky coefficient, projection tolerance/iterations, and optional aerodynamic reference values. `Build CFD Grid + Run` reconstructs the static hierarchy, initializes pressure, and immediately starts continuous CFD; Play/Pause and Step control the resulting worker. Successful STEP/config loads are persisted in a ten-entry `File -> Recent files` menu; an ordinary no-argument startup restores the most recent existing wing/config and its confirmed placement, but deliberately does not build or run CFD. Missing recent paths are pruned. A clearly labelled cropped-Y diagnostic clips the oriented open fabric to a selectable span station and requested physical Y width, rounds the width upward to an integer number of configured finest cells, selects a Y-normal 2-D view, and displays the cell count/current step prominently. It uses the real uniform EB/pressure/timestep path but is a collapse-localization tool, not a physical whole-wing result; its runtime crop and uniform-grid overrides are not serialized into the normal wing config. The internal freestream velocity remains +X, so the leading edge must face upstream toward -X. On direct STEP load, the longer horizontal face-only bbox axis is treated as span and the shorter as chord; the current exporter convention assumes the negative chord direction points toward the leading edge and maps it to upstream -X. A prominent 180-degree LE/TE flip remains because bbox extents cannot determine chord polarity. Config files store the confirmed placement matrix and do not re-infer it. GUI and command-line preprocessing share one brick-aligned zero-origin framing helper; placement is canonicalized to the FP32 display/production-mesh frame before clipping so both paths build identical EB topology. EB atlas geometry halos are clamped at physical-domain boundaries, so a small fragment cannot merge into a nonphysical halo cell when fabric meets a cropped-domain boundary.

## Build and test

The RTX 4090 development configuration is:

```powershell
cmake -S . -B build -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DCMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=89
cmake --build build
ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure
```

The repository may already contain a Visual Studio build directory; preserve and use its established generator rather than reconfiguring it in place.

Important probes are:

- `paraglider_geometry_probe`: deterministic geometry, EB topology, pressure/operator, AMR exchange, FP32 parity, and load tests;
- `paraglider_gpu_probe`: CUDA pressure-operator timing and allocation estimates;
- `paraglider_flow_probe`: dynamic normal/parallel/inclined plates, projection divergence, opened-cavity connectivity, and AMR-versus-uniform force comparison;
- `paraglider_probe`: STEP/config preprocessing diagnostics;
- `paraglider_case_probe`: imported-wing multi-step force, CFL, conservation, and performance history (manual long-running diagnostic);
- `parity_probe`: CPU/GPU parity and immutable snapshots for the reusable FP64 uniform-MAC reference kernels.

`paraglider_case_probe --physical-time T --max-levels N` runs an imported wing to a common physical time instead of comparing arbitrary step counts. If `--steps` is also given it is a hard safety limit and failure to reach `T` returns nonzero. A final `[paraglider-case-summary]` line is stable key/value output for convergence scripts; velocity maxima on that line are explicitly labelled pre-step because they are the values used to choose the final CFL timestep.

`paraglider_case_probe --thin-y-fraction 0.5 --thin-y-width 1.0` reproduces the GUI's cropped-Y centre-span diagnostic. Width is in metres and snaps upward to whole finest cells; the default remains 0.125 m (two cells for the checked-in PlanB configuration).

`configs/paraglider.json` is the new configuration reference.
`configs/planb_parakite.json` is the checked-in PlanB acceptance case; its -90-degree Z placement maps the confirmed leading-edge direction (-Y) to ParaCFD upstream (-X), against the +X freestream velocity.

## Current limitations

ParaCFD is now a paraglider-only application, but it must not yet be described as producing trustworthy end-to-end paraglider aerodynamic results:

- EB that reaches a 2:1 interface is currently rejected instead of receiving aperture-aware cross-level fragment reconstruction. The tested PlanB hierarchy keeps its EB atlas away from these interfaces.
- The composite solver has a two-level additive geometric/Galerkin preconditioner, not yet a complete recursive multigrid hierarchy. The checked-in PlanB initial projection converges at the configured global residual tolerance, but local maximum divergence remains sensitive to the very small irregular control volumes and needs a stricter local/conservation acceptance criterion.
- The new external-aero timestep is wired end to end with bounded MacCormack/RK2 advection. Fabric-near faces use a precomputed same-side link graph with minmod-limited linear transport where the full connected stencil exists, a first-order fallback at incomplete stencils, and link-restricted molecular diffusion. Same-level brick crossings and normal 2:1 interface fluxes are synchronized conservatively. Cross-level face/cell prolongation is now linearly exact to the FP32 geometry-coordinate floor, but semi-Lagrangian momentum advection is still not a globally conservative refluxed finite-volume update.
- A separate one-level finite-volume MAC kernel is the conservative-momentum foundation. It evaluates each upwind momentum flux symmetrically at both endpoints, preserves constant vector fields, conserves the discrete lattice momentum across same-level brick boundaries, and suppresses links cut by fabric. Its tested external-aero variant imports prescribed +X freestream momentum, exports the interior donor state at X-max, applies zero normal mass flux at the Y/Z free-slip boundaries, and uses exact half-width boundary staggered volumes and edge/corner areas. A uniform field remains uniform and a localized transverse packet exports exactly `dt U A` without lateral leakage in both FP32 and FP64. A geometry-independent CPU/GPU pairwise transport twin defines the composite contract: each open connection applies one shared upwind momentum transfer with exactly opposite endpoint increments, component-by-component on the staggered layout. Regular staggered dual volume is assembled from one half of every active adjacent pressure cell; a normal 2:1 tile exactly replaces the missing covered-side portion with one quarter of the coarse half-cell plus the fine half-cell. Tangential links intersect actual staggered dual-face rectangles, canonicalize duplicate same-level faces, and retain their exact pressure-tile overlap sources rather than assuming four links. One unified ownership graph combines normal and tangential records so a physical face at several intersecting refinement boundaries remains one state. The fixture has 7,023 canonical nodes, 7,840 transfers, and 501 multi-axis nodes; it conserves each component to `1.47e-14`. A persistent GPU driver gathers those nodes from pooled per-level MAC SoA arrays, derives normal advectors from owned MAC states and tangential advectors from 6,144 area-weighted GPU-resident projected pressure-flux contributions, advances the pairwise graph, scatters the owned states, and rebuilds 384 coarse normal-face aliases. Its pressure-flux-derived state/alias errors are `5.70e-8 / 4.08e-8` FP32 and zero FP64. An alias that is also independently owned is rejected explicitly. Static preprocessing clears every replaced directed structured link (7,920 in the fixture), preventing regular/compact double counting. `ExternalAeroCore` does not call the composite update yet; compact EB coupling and multilevel physical-boundary ownership are still required before production advection is switched.
- Compact EB aperture velocities use bounded minmod-limited MUSCL transport on complete same-axis graph chains, with a first-order donor fallback where a chain ends or branches, plus molecular diffusion and component-collocated weighted least-squares reconstruction of the full velocity-gradient tensor. Conservative 0.25-volume merging and the reportable `1e-4 h^2` aperture cutoff remove numerical slivers. Tests cover a smooth quadratic characteristic, uniform-state preservation, local bounds, LES dissipation, tangential shear, rigid rotation, normal extension, exact affine reconstruction, winding-invariant inclined no-slip damping, wall flux on a face-aligned membrane with no compact apertures, and production Spalding smooth-wall flux/traction in both viscous and high-Re regimes. Grid/domain/force convergence is still required before trusting loads.
- The earlier compact-momentum validation operator reconstructs fragment vectors from aperture and physical-carrier half masses. It proves equal/opposite internal transfer (`1.19e-7` FP32, zero FP64), exact perimeter mass-flux ownership, and unique same-level carrier aliases, but it is not a promotable regular/EB mortar: composing its truncated regular-carrier ring with the structured update changes a uniform carrier state. That failed free-stream gate is why `ExternalAeroCore` does not call it. The replacement stores one vector momentum state on each actual pressure control volume (ordinary cell or split fragment), while the projected MAC/aperture velocities remain the mass-flux DOFs. Ordinary same-level connections run through one implicit brick CUDA kernel per level; only 2:1 tiles and regular/compact EB connections are materialized, so this is not a full-domain sparse graph. The same upwind flux updates both endpoint volumes. The one-level membrane fixture preserves freestream exactly and closes CPU/GPU conservation plus analytical external export. On the three-level fixture, 1,536 coarse/fine pressure-flux tiles preserve freestream exactly; a perturbed state changes by `8.18e-4`, has CPU/GPU state error `1.49e-8` in FP32 (zero FP64), and conserves integrated momentum to `1.42e-7` FP32 storage rounding / `4.44e-15` FP64. External boundary ownership uses explicit physical-domain flags, preventing an internal coarse/fine interface with no same-level neighbour from becoming a false inlet/outlet. Molecular diffusion reuses the same open-face graph and pressure-operator conductance `A n·d/|d|²`: EB/multilevel cases dissipate energy, conserve to `7.45e-8 / 2.39e-9` FP32 (`2.84e-14 / 1.11e-14` FP64), and cannot mix constant states across a face-aligned sheet. This new state is validation-only until same-side LES and the complete production timestep are connected.
- On the current 47,997-triangle PlanB working fixture with the confirmed leading edge facing upstream, the conditioned build has 78,299 full-rank fragment/component stencils and 23,105 wall-side control volumes. The production smooth-wall 1,500-step gate reaches 3.831115 s with 12.35/15.57 m/s regular/compact peaks, pressure force `[173.09, -4.73, -34.56]` N, viscous force `[7.16, -0.024, 0.033]` N, and total force `[180.25, -4.76, -34.53]` N. Max/volume-weighted-RMS divergence is `9.16e-4 / 5.18e-6 s^-1`, net integrated flux error is `-6.31e-5 m^3/s`, and persistent GPU storage is 521.11 MiB. The last step is 60.3 ms including 17.7 ms projection and 0.34 ms compact/wall transport. It remains stable without an absolute velocity guard. Loads remain grid-dependent; full grid/domain/force convergence and AoA confirmation remain necessary.
- Cell-to-flux reconstruction and reverse pressure coupling are implemented without a smoothing round trip. Ordinary faces remain implicit; compact regular/fragment, 2:1, and EB faces interpolate the normal component at the real face position between actual control-volume centroids. CPU/GPU reconstruction errors are `1.49e-8` FP32 and roundoff FP64, including all 1,536 multilevel tiles. A face-aligned membrane remains exactly zero-flux with opposite side states. Composite projection reduces a deliberately divergent reconstruction from `1.4` to about `4.5e-5 s^-1` in FP32 (`3.83e-11` FP64). Its GPU pressure acts directly on persistent control-volume momentum: open faces get paired opposite finite-volume impulses and fabric applies `+p_plus A n` / `-p_minus A n` to its two fluid sides, exactly opposite the winding-invariant CAD load. The closed two-pressure fixture balances pointwise; the 1,536-tile impulse conserves to `3.74e-9` FP32 / `5.12e-15` FP64 with CPU/GPU error `1.67e-8` / `2.78e-17`.
- The first equal-physical-time PlanB gate at about 0.100 s gives pressure-only `[Fx,Fz]` of `[180.2,-8.17]`, `[200.6,-5.25]`, and `[203.3,-24.77]` N at 0.125, 0.0625, and 0.03125 m finest spacing. Streamwise force changes 11.3% then 1.4%, but vertical force is plainly unconverged and the flow is still transient. These values establish a reproducible blocker; they are not an aerodynamic result.
- The Qt viewer displays live velocity/pressure slices, arrows, fabric-stopped tracers, AMR/EB boxes, and triangle-native visible-side Cp, Cp+, Cp-, or delta-Cp. The scalar slice is finest-brick aware and its per-axis sample counts follow finest cell spacing, but visualization still uses throttled host snapshots and OpenGL uploads rather than direct CUDA/OpenGL interop; arrows and tracers use a coarse uniform 3-D display copy.
- The GPU Spalding smooth-wall flux affects production flow and publishes separate viscous and total loads. Its smooth-wall assumption has not been calibrated for a real fabric roughness, and the whole solver still lacks grid/domain/force convergence.
- The opened-cavity gate measures developed bidirectional flow through a deliberately missing fabric face while the closed control has zero represented opening flux. Longer internal-pressure and force-convergence studies on resolved paraglider geometry remain outstanding.

The former building, binary-solid voxel, ground/channel, seabed, porous, scene, and building GUI paths have been removed. Only mathematically reusable uniform-MAC FP64 kernels remain as validation references; they are not linked into the production external-aero timestep.
