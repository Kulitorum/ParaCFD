# 3D Packed-Fraction Representation of Sediment (Deposits Inside Hollow Shapes)

## Overview

A single-valued Exner heightfield z_b(x,y) cannot represent sand that settles under roofs, behind perforated walls, or inside cavities of our printed units — which is the entire product mechanism. The published precedent for the alternative is FLOW-3D's sediment scour model (Brethour 2003 FSI-03-TN62; Brethour & Burnham 2010 FSI-10-TN85; Wei, Brethour, Grünzner & Burnham 2014, Flow Science Report FSR-03-14): the packed bed is a **per-cell solid volume fraction** (FAVOR area/volume fractions), cells solidify at a **critical packing fraction 0.64**, and — decisive for us — *"If no packed bed exists initially, a geometry component is automatically generated by the code to represent the packed bed when it is formed during simulation"* (FSR-03-14, Sec. 1). Deposition inside/around arbitrary structures is native; their Gladstone (1998) settling-tank validation reproduced deposit-density distributions on 1.9 cm cells with all-default constants. The Eulerian two-phase route (SedFoam-2.0, Chauchat et al. 2017) supplies the rigorous closures for flow through partially packed cells (drag, hindrance, frictional pressure) that FSR-03-14 leaves implicit. This note assembles a complete fluid/suspended/interface/packed/printed-solid **cell-state machine** with all equations, and the rules the heightfield notes (03, 10) could not give: bedload and 32/30-degree avalanching on a fraction field, re-erosion of packed cells, bed shear in cavities where the log-law probe z_p = max(1.5 dz, 2 ks) exceeds the clearance, and minimum cells-per-orifice.

**Decision: adopt a single 3D packed-fraction field f_pack on the voxel grid (FLOW-3D architecture), NOT a heightfield, and not a hybrid.** In open-bed columns the fraction update degenerates exactly to Exner (see Eq. 6), so nothing from notes 03/10 is lost; a hybrid (heightfield in open bed + fraction near obstacles) creates a seam where mass conservation and avalanching must be hand-stitched — reject it.

## Key equations & results

Constants throughout: rho_f = 1025 kg/m3, rho_s = 2650 kg/m3, s = 2.585, nu = 1.05e-6 m2/s, mu = 1.076e-3 Pa·s, g = 9.81 m/s2, kappa = 0.40.

### 1. Cell composition and states

Per cell: static printed-solid fraction phi_obs in [0,1] (voxelized STL); packed-grain volume fraction f_pack (grains only, pore water excluded); suspended volumetric concentration c_n per species (m3 grain / m3 mixture). Critical packing fraction **c_pack = 0.64** (FLOW-3D default, = random close packing; legitimate range 0.55–0.70; SedFoam uses alpha_max = 0.635 for spheres) [FSR-03-14 applications; Chauchat et al. 2017 Eq. 25]. Bed porosity n = 1 − c_pack = 0.36, consistent with the Exner factor (1 − n) = 0.64 in note 03. Define the **normalized fill** F = f_pack / (c_pack · (1 − phi_obs)) in [0,1]. States: `PRINTED` (phi_obs ≈ 1, immutable), `PACKED` (F = 1, solid for the fluid solver, erodible where exposed), `INTERFACE` (0 < F < 1), `FLUID` (F = 0, carries c_n). Open volume fraction for the flow solver: V_open = (1 − phi_obs)(1 − F).

### 2. Interface geometry (replaces "bed surface" everywhere)

Outward bed normal and bed area per cell from the smoothed fill field (identical role to FAVOR interface reconstruction, FSR-03-14 Sec. 1):

    n_b = −grad(F~)/|grad(F~)|,   A_b = |grad(F~)| · V_cell,   F~ = 3x3x3 box-filtered F

Bed slope angle beta = angle between n_b and vertical = acos(n_b · z_hat). All formulas of notes 01/03/04/10 that referenced "the bed surface z_b" now evaluate on the set of INTERFACE cells using (n_b, A_b, beta) — including surfaces on cavity ceilings and vertical deposit faces.

### 3. Deposition source (suspended → packed), with hindered settling

Suspended sediment advects with u_s,n = u_bar + (1 − c_tot) · w_s,n · g_hat (drift-flux; the (1 − c_tot) factor is the mass-consistent form — FSR-03-14 Eq. 22 prints u_s = u_bar + u_settle·c_s, which does not reduce to u_s = u_f + w_s in the dilute limit; treat that as a misprint and use the form above). At every INTERFACE cell (and at FLUID cells whose settling-direction face is PACKED/PRINTED/domain floor), the deposition flux is:

    D_n = w_s,n(hindered) · c_n(cell above interface)     [m3 grain / m2 / s]
    df_pack/dt |_dep = + (A_b / V_cell) · sum_n D_n

Clear-water w_s from Soulsby (1997): w_s = (nu/d)(sqrt(10.36^2 + 1.049 D*^3) − 10.36) = 0.0073 / 0.024 / 0.118 / 0.40 m/s for d50 = 0.1 / 0.2 / 1 / 10 mm (note 10, verified). Hindered settling (Richardson & Zaki; HEC-RAS 2D Sediment Technical Reference):

    w_s(hindered) = w_s0 · (1 − c_tot)^m,  m = 4.0 default (range 3.75–4.45 for 0.05–0.5 mm sand; classic RZ: 4.65 for Re_p < 0.2, 2.4 for Re_p > 500); apply for c_tot > ~0.001 (3000 mg/L).

Sanity number: c = 0.002 (5.3 g/L) of 0.2 mm sand deposits grain volume at 4.8e-5 m/s → the bed (bulk) rises at 4.8e-5/0.64 = 7.5e-5 m/s ≈ **0.27 m/h** inside a still cavity.

### 4. Entrainment / re-erosion of packed cells (packed → suspended)

Winterwerp et al. (1992) lifting velocity, applied along n_b at every INTERFACE cell (FSR-03-14 Eq. 10 — this is *the* erosion law for a fraction field; there is no pickup-function ambiguity):

    u_lift,n = alpha_n · D*_n^0.3 · (theta_n − theta'_cr,n)^1.5 · sqrt(g d_n (s − 1)),  alpha_n = 0.018
    df_pack/dt |_ero = − (A_b / V_cell) · u_lift,n · c_pack · c_b,n     (bed recedes at u_lift)

theta from the cavity-aware shear probe (item 8); theta'_cr slope-corrected (Soulsby 1997; FSR-03-14 Eq. 9) using beta from n_b, phi = 32 deg; **guard theta'_cr >= 0.1·theta_cr** so overhanging normals (beta > phi, where the formula goes to/below zero) do not cause instant mass ejection — avalanching (item 6) is responsible for those. Entrainment and deposition run simultaneously; the net is the exchange (FSR-03-14 Sec. 2.5). Example: theta = 2·theta_cr, d = 0.2 mm → u_lift = 1.7e-5 m/s = 6.3 cm/h of bed recession — right order for clear-water scour.

### 5. Bedload on the interface manifold (FLOW-3D "sub-mesh" method)

MPM (FSR-03-14 Eqs. 12–17), per species: Phi_n = B_n (theta_n − theta'_cr,n)^1.5 c_b,n, B_n = 8.0 (5.0–5.7 low transport, 13.0 sheet flow); q_b,n = Phi_n · sqrt(g (s−1) d_n^3) [m2/s], directed along the flow tangential to the interface: t_hat = (u_p − (u_p·n_b) n_b)/|...| with u_p the probe velocity. Bedload layer thickness h_n = 0.3 d_n D*^0.7 (theta/theta_cr − 1)^0.5; bedload advection velocity u_b,n = q_b,n/(h_n c_b,n f_b) with f_b = total packing fraction = 0.64 (FSR-03-14 Eq. 17). Implementation on the fraction field: for each INTERFACE cell, compute the vector flux Q_n = q_b,n · t_hat · L_face and exchange f_pack with the **downwind adjacent INTERFACE cell** (upwind donor scheme over the 6-face + 12-edge neighborhood restricted to interface cells):

    df_pack/dt |_bl = − div_manifold(q_b) · (A_b / V_cell)

When a cell reaches F = 1 it flips to PACKED and the adjacent cell along +n_b becomes INTERFACE (deposition front advances); at F = 0 it flips to FLUID and the cell along −n_b is exposed. Worked example (d = 0.2 mm, theta = 0.10, theta_cr = 0.049): q_b = 1.0e-6 m2/s, h_b = 0.18 mm, u_b = 8.7e-3 m/s; time to transit one 2.5 cm cell ≈ 3 s; time to fill one cell face ≈ dx·dz·c_pack/q_b ≈ 400 s → morphological time step is never bedload-limited at our rates.

### 6. Exner equivalence (why nothing from notes 03/10 is lost)

Summing item 3–5 over a vertical column of cells in open bed (single interface crossing) gives exactly

    c_pack · dz_b/dt = −div_h(q_b) + D − E,  i.e. (1 − n) dz_b/dt = −div(q_b) + D − E with n = 0.36,

the Exner equation of note 03/10 — the fraction field is a strict superset of the heightfield.

### 7. Avalanching (32/30 deg) on layered columns

A fraction field under overhangs is equivalent to a **multi-layer heightfield**: per (x,y) column, extract the ordered list of bed segments (maximal runs of PACKED/INTERFACE cells, each with a top surface z_top = z_base + sum(F·dz)); open bed = 1 segment; under a roof = 2+ segments (layered terrain representation, Beneš & Forsbach 2001). Avalanche each segment's *top surface* against the corresponding surface of the 8 horizontal neighbor segments **whose vertical interval overlaps and with no PRINTED cell blocking the straight path between the column pair**:

    if (z_top,i − z_top,j) > L_ij · tan(32 deg):  move dV = k_av · 0.5 · A_col · (z_top,i − z_top,j − L_ij · tan(30 deg))

with L_ij = horizontal center distance (dx or dx·sqrt(2)), A_col = dx^2, k_av = 0.5 (relaxation), iterating Gauss–Seidel sweeps until all slopes < 30 deg (trigger 32 deg / release 30 deg hysteresis, Roulund-type, as verified in note 10). Moved volume lands as f_pack in the receiving segment's top interface cell; a receiving segment whose gap to the roof above is < 1 cell just fills to the roof (F = 1 against phi_obs). Volume is conserved exactly per pair transfer. This is the piece FLOW-3D does *not* publish (it relies on slope-corrected theta_cr only, and its dam test shows slope sliding emerging from that); the explicit sweep is required for our LES + Exner-type update (regularization, note 10 item 8).

### 8. Flow through partially packed cells + bed shear in cavities

**Packed cells are hydraulically solid.** Justification: Ergun (1952) permeability K_perm = eps^3 d^2/(150 (1−eps)^2), eps = 0.36 → K_perm = 3.0e-11 m2 (d50 = 0.2 mm) to 7.6e-8 m2 (10 mm); under a strong gradient of 0.5·rho·U^2 per meter (512 Pa/m at U = 1 m/s) Darcy seepage is 1.4e-5 m/s (sand) — negligible. Enforce u = 0 in PACKED and PRINTED cells (mask in advection + projection).

**INTERFACE cells (0 < F < 1)**: apply an Ergun/Darcy–Forchheimer sink scaled by the local grain fraction alpha = F·c_pack:

    f_drag/V = −[ 150 · mu · alpha^2 / ((1−alpha)^2 d^2) + 1.75 · rho_f · alpha / ((1−alpha) d) · |u| ] · u / (1−alpha)

(equivalently SedFoam's dilute-side drag K = 0.75 C_d (rho_f/d) |u_r| (1−alpha)^(−2.65), C_d Schiller–Naumann, hindrance exponent hExp = 2.65, Ergun branch for alpha > 0.2 — Chauchat et al. 2017 Eqs. 5–6). Implement implicitly (u_new = u/(1 + dt·K/rho)) — this ramps velocity to zero smoothly as F→1 and kills stair-step flicker at the solidification threshold. Optional suspended-phase stiffening: mu_mix = mu_f (1 − c_tot/0.64)^(−1.6) (Krieger–Dougherty, intrinsic viscosity 2.5 × 0.64; FLOW-3D's legacy model used the near-identical exponent −1.55). We do NOT need SedFoam's frictional pressure p_ff = Fr(alpha − 0.57)^3/(0.635 − alpha)^5, Fr = 0.05 Pa (Johnson–Jackson; Chauchat et al. 2017 Eq. 25) — that machinery exists to *hold the bed static in a two-phase solver*; our hard solidification at 0.64 does that by construction.

**Bed-shear probe inside cavities** (clearance H_c = raycast distance from interface cell along n_b to the first PACKED/PRINTED cell; interior clearances 0.1–1 m, dz = 2.5–5 cm):
- H_c >= 4·z_p0 (z_p0 = max(1.5 dz, 2 ks), note 04): standard log-law probe.
- 6 dz <= H_c < 4·z_p0: probe at z_p = H_c/4, log-law with z0 = ks/30 unchanged (log layer fills ~the lower half of a rough channel).
- 3 dz <= H_c < 6 dz: fully-developed-gap estimate: sample U_gap at mid-gap (H_c/2), tau_b = rho_f [kappa/ln(H_c/(2 z0))]^2 U_gap^2 if Re_gap = U_gap·H_c/nu > 2800, else laminar plane-Poiseuille tau_b = 6 mu U_gap / H_c.
- H_c < 3 dz: the gap flow is numerically meaningless — freeze erosion (compute deposition only) and, if the gap is a designed perforation, model it as the sub-grid porous screen of note 07: momentum sink 0.5·rho·k·u|u| per screen thickness with **k = 1/beta_por^2 − 1** (Taylor–Davies; beta_por = open-area ratio), letting suspended sediment pass with settling intact.

**Minimum cells per orifice** (design-of-experiments rule, heuristic — no single citable constant exists): >= 8–10 cells across a perforation diameter for jet/discharge-coefficient fidelity within ~5–10% (orifice mesh-independence studies converge around this); 4–7 cells = qualitative only (flag output); < 3–4 cells = must use the k = 1/beta_por^2 − 1 screen model. At dz = 2.5 cm this means resolved perforations >= 0.2–0.25 m; smaller print features are sub-grid by definition.

### 9. Mass bookkeeping (three reservoirs, one identity)

Grain volume only (pore water never counted): V_bed = sum(f_pack · V_cell); V_susp = sum_n(c_n · V_open,cell); bedload has zero storage (flux only, FSR-03-14 sub-mesh). Every exchange is written as a pair of equal/opposite cell updates (D, E, div q_b, avalanche transfers), so

    d/dt (V_bed + V_susp) = boundary influx − boundary outflux   (machine precision; audit every ~1000 steps)

Conversions: grain volume → bulk bed volume: divide by c_pack = 0.64; deposited *mass* per cell = f_pack · V_cell · 2650 kg/m3 (this is the self-ballasting KPI); equivalent Exner height change per column: dz_b = d(sum f_pack · dz)/c_pack. Guard against gelling in dead-end cavities: cap c_tot at 0.35; any excess converts directly to f_pack at the cell's supporting face (our closure — mark in code as heuristic).

## Practical guidance for our simulator

**State machine (per cell, evaluated each morphological step, order matters):**
1. Flow substeps with V_open mask + interface Ergun drag (item 8); accumulate time-averaged probe velocities.
2. tau_b/theta on INTERFACE cells via cavity-aware probe; theta'_cr from n_b slope.
3. Exchange pass: D (item 3), E (item 4), bedload donor transfer (item 5) — all as f_pack/c_n increments.
4. Avalanche sweeps on layered columns until slopes < 30 deg (item 7).
5. State flips: F >= 1 → PACKED (zero velocity, remove from c-advection); F <= 0 → FLUID; expose/promote neighbors along ±n_b; recompute F~, n_b, A_b.
6. Mass audit (item 9).

**Memory/GPU cost**: one extra float (f_pack) + one uint8 state per cell = ~5 bytes/cell → 150 MB at 30 M cells; the interface set is ~N^(2/3) ≈ 10^5 cells, so items 2–5 are cheap relative to the LES. No mesh motion, no re-voxelization — only mask updates.

**Defaults**: c_pack = 0.64; m_hindered = 4.0; alpha_lift = 0.018; B_MPM = 8.0 (switch to van Rijn 1984 both-branch for d50 <= 2 mm per note 10); phi = 32/30 deg; k_av = 0.5; theta'_cr floor 0.1·theta_cr; c_tot cap 0.35; ks = 2.5 d50; probe rules of item 8.

**Validation ladder specific to this representation**: (1) Gladstone et al. (1998) bidisperse settling tank (deposit density along tank, 25/69 um SiC — FLOW-3D matched with defaults on 1.9 cm cells); (2) Chatterjee (1994) submerged-jet scour (d50 = 0.76 mm, theta_cr = 0.05 — scour hole + dune within experimental envelope); (3) a roofed-box trap of our own design: verify deposited mass equals settling-flux integral to < 1% (bookkeeping identity), and that an open-bed run reproduces the Exner/heightfield result of notes 03/10 cell-for-cell.

**What we deliberately did NOT adopt**: full two-phase SedFoam (kinetic theory + mu(I)) — physically superior for sheet flow but ~100x our budget at 10 m/30 M cells; hybrid heightfield+fraction (seam bugs); porous flow through the packed bed (negligible, item 8).
